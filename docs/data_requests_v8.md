**Title:** Data availability and wavelength convention for Fe & Al / BAM310 (D4JA00377B)

**Body:**

Hello,

I am looking for the measured Fe & Al dataset described in sections 2.3–2.5 of
“On the non-universality of distance metrics in laser-induced breakdown
spectroscopy” (DOI: 10.1039/D4JA00377B), particularly the BAM310 observations.
I checked the linked Fe–Co/soil releases, the public spectra_similarity_datasets
folder and the article's ESI, but have not located these numerical Fe & Al files.

Could you point to an existing release or provide the following existing records?

1. The numerical BAM310 files and an explicit licence or permission for their reuse.
2. The wavelength convention of the released axis (air or vacuum), with the
   applicable calibration/export or conversion description.
3. The link between the files and the actual BAM310 specimen/preparation, and
   whether the ablated positions lie in the certificate's valid region (excluding
   the outer 5 mm and the central 20 mm diameter). A documented statement or
   existing acquisition map would be sufficient.
4. Any existing specimen, preparation and acquisition identifiers needed to keep
   repeated positions/laser energies grouped as one material in an external test.

The intended use is a test of a fixed model on a chemically characterised
low-Fe control. Repeated laser positions would be reported as repeats of that
material. No new measurements are requested.

Thank you.

**Subject:** GOE100 stock provenance and Mg LA-ICP-MS quantification records

**Body:**

Hello,

I am checking whether GOE100 in the LANL release (Zenodo 3902232; compositions
in Zenodo 3903074) can serve as an independent low-Mg control for an external
evaluation. The eight goethite LA-ICP-MS spots in Figure C.3 of the thesis give
mean MgO about 0.00732 wt%. I have not located the analytical QA or the source
records resolving its relationship to GoeDV in the PDS ChemCam calibration set
(for example the product 2018_07_30_134057_CCS).

Could you clarify these two points using existing records?

1. Do GOE100 and GoeDV share an original powder, supplier batch, specimen or
   preparation family? If they differ, is there a source or preparation record
   linking the assayed endmember and the released GOE100 files to the distinct
   material? Different file names or measurement dates alone are not sufficient
   for the intended material-level independence check.
2. Which calibration/internal standard, normalisation and quantitative QA apply
   to these Mg LA-ICP-MS measurements? Any existing recovery, reference-material,
   detection/quantification-limit or uncertainty information applicable to this
   matrix would help establish a defensible MgO upper bound relative to 0.1 wt%.
   A formal manufacturer CoA or a bound on every grain is not required.

The LANL CCS vacuum/nm processing lineage is already documented. No new spectra
or measurements are requested; the question concerns existing provenance and
assay records.

Thank you.
